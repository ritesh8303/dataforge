In plain English

What is DataForge?

DataForge is a free job finder for students and juniors looking for data and AI roles in Europe — internships, working-student (Werkstudent) jobs, thesis topics, and junior positions.

Jobs come from public sources (German BA Jobsuche, EURES, company career pages, and a few open job boards). We do not scrape LinkedIn or Indeed.

How to use it

  • Jobs — browse and filter (start here)
  • Match my resume — paste your CV for a short suggested list
  • Charts — optional market counts

Important limits

  • A listing here does not guarantee the role is still open — always check the company website
  • Match scores are suggestions, not hiring decisions
  • Visa / language flags are hints from the job text — not legal advice

Who built this

Built by Ritesh Rakesh Jadhav as a portfolio project (and M.Sc. Data Science thesis packaging) to show real data + AI engineering — while staying useful for early-career seekers.

Technical details (for recruiters / engineers)
  • Public APIs → AWS Bronze / Silver / Gold lakehouse → Jobs + Match APIs → this website
  • Early-career filter (default): EU × data/AI fields × fresher / working student / internship / thesis
  • Worldwide remote filter: remote × data/AI × early-career (any country)
  • Match uses hybrid search; eval scores use synthetic labels unless human labels are added
  • Governance: Responsible AI
