\section{Layer contracts (non-negotiable for RQ1)}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Bronze} may not invent business KPIs.
  \item \textbf{Silver} owns identity and SCD validity; GenAI must not mutate \texttt{job\_id} / SCD keys.
  \item \textbf{Gold} may consume additive enrichment joins.
  \item Quality gates (Pydantic ingest, region taxonomy, CI quality script, dbt tests) remain authoritative.
\end{itemize}

\section{Heuristic Career Matching Wizard (baseline for RQ3)}

The pre-AI wizard scores candidates with a \textbf{60\% skills / 20\% seniority / 20\% location} heuristic. It is an honest non-neural baseline---useful scientifically precisely because it is simple and transparent.

\section{Audience refinement for the thesis product}

The Match surface targets \textbf{intern / working-student / junior} seekers with optional visa-aware filters (e.g.\ Chancenkarte / job-seeker friendly heuristics). Visa fields are \textbf{advisory}, evidence-cited, and never legal advice.
