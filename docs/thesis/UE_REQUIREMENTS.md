## Layout (binding)

- Margins: 2 cm left, 4 cm right, 2.5 cm top, 3 cm bottom.
- Font: Arial **or** Times New Roman **or** Cambria, 12 pt body, 10 pt footnotes, black.
- Justify; 6 pt paragraph spacing; 1 cm indent.
- DIN 1421 numbering; not deeper than 2.3.4.5.
- Print one side only, 80 g white A4.
- Harvard name–date citations (author, year, page). Direct quotes in `" "`. Paraphrases: `cf.`
- Direct quotes reproduce the original **including errors**.
- Bibliography: one alphabetical list; up to three authors with `;`; more than three: first author *et al.*
- Online sources: author, title, URL or DOI, **access date**.
- Equations: italic parameters, numbered `(4.1)` on the right; “where” is not capitalised.

## Thesis body (combine both structure slides)

1. Cover sheet  
2. Statutory declaration (lecture wording is in `latex/overleaf_main.tex`)  
3. Table of contents  
4. List of abbreviations, tables, figures  
5. Abstract  
6. Introduction  
7. Theoretical background / literature (**major share**)  
8. Research questions  
9. Methodology (defend *why this method*)  
10. Results  
11. Discussion (own opinions, practical application)  
12. Implications and future research  
13. Bibliography  

Literature source hierarchy: peer-reviewed journals → IEEE/Sage/Springer/Elsevier/Blackwell/Taylor & Francis → monographs → internet (not recommended).
